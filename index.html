You don't pick your event. You discover it.

A running career game for iPhone and iPad. Start as an unknown with hidden strengths, plan every week, race it live — and find out whether you're a miler, a marathoner or a mountain runner.

Download on the App Store Free · No ads · Plays offline
The game

A whole career, one week at a time

Every week you set the training and choose the races. Every race tells you a little more about the athlete you really have.

Discover

Hidden talent

Attributes and traits are revealed by racing, not menus. Expected vs. actual separates a good day from real ability.

Plan

One week, one decision

Volume and key sessions, tapers and altitude camps — or let the coach plan. Fatigue, form and injury risk behave like a real training block.

Race

Live tactics

Push, hold or ease while the race unfolds. Sit in the pack and kick, or lead from the gun. Rivals remember every meeting.

Climb

To the world stage

Regional cup, national series, national and world rankings, team selection and championships on a living calendar.

Measure

Athlete Lab

VO2max, VDOT, lactate thresholds, heart-rate zones, HRV and race predictions — calibrated against real-world records.

Remember

Legacy

Medals, cups and legendary trophies, the Hall of Fame, Masters age groups, posters, a Legacy Book and the What If? Lab.

Fair by design

  • The whole game is free, from the first race to the last season
  • No timers, no energy, no ads, no expiring offers
  • Everything is unlocked with Laurels you earn by playing
  • Optional Laurel packs only speed things up
  • Gear boxes are won in races and never sold
  • No account, no tracking, no data collected

100 Game Center achievements

From your first finish to goals that take decades of careers.

Offline, with optional iCloud

Your careers live on your device; turn on iCloud sync to continue on another one.
