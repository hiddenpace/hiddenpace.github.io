Frequently asked questions

Purchases and Laurels

I bought a Laurel pack but the Laurels didn't arrive

Open the in-game Store and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom. Every purchase is delivered exactly once per transaction, so restoring never adds Laurels twice. If a purchase shows "waiting for approval" (Ask to Buy), the Laurels arrive as soon as it's approved.

Why did my first pack give double Laurels — and the second didn't?

The first Laurel pack bought on an Apple Account counts double. It's a one-time welcome bonus, not a timed offer, and it is shown on the Store tiles as ×2 until you use it.

Do I have to pay to enjoy the game?

No. The whole game is free, and everything in the Store — modules, gear, posters, extra career slots — can be unlocked with Laurels earned by playing (season goals, discoveries, achievements, finished careers). Packs only make it faster. There are no timers, energy, ads or expiring offers.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through the App Store, so refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and choose the purchase. If a refund is granted, the Laurels from that pack are removed from your balance.

Saves, iCloud and devices

How do I continue on another iPhone or iPad?

Turn on iCloud sync in the game's Settings on both devices, signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account. The change takes effect on the next launch. If both devices played the same career, the one that is further along wins.

The game said "Career restored" — what happened?

Your latest save couldn't be read (for example, after the device ran out of storage), so the game automatically restored the career from one of its weekly backup copies. You may need to replay a few weeks.

I deleted the app. Is my progress gone?

If iCloud sync was on, reinstall the app and turn iCloud sync on again — your careers come back. Without iCloud, saves live only on the device and are removed with the app. Laurel purchases can always be restored from the Store.

Game Center and achievements

My achievements don't show up in Game Center

Make sure you're signed in to Game Center (iOS Settings → Game Center). Achievements unlocked earlier are sent the next time you open the game while signed in. Achievement Laurels are credited in the game whether or not you use Game Center.

Playing

How do I change the language or units?

In the game's Settings you can switch between 15 languages, kilometres or miles, and Celsius or Fahrenheit. You can also change the app language in iOS Settings → Hidden Pace → Language.

Is Hidden Pace a training app?

No — it's a game. The simulation is calibrated against real-world records and training research, but its advice is part of the game, not medical or coaching guidance.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. It plays fully offline. The internet is only used by Apple services you choose: the App Store, iCloud sync and Game Center.
